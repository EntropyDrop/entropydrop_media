I'm building EntropyDrop Space, a programmable voxel sandbox game that runs in your browser. In this devlog, I use Agent Build to create three playable machines, customize them with voxel tools, and put their physics to the test across three different worlds.

• Aether Archipelago: Build and fly a tandem-rotor Chinook helicopter between floating islands.
• Nature: Create an off-road rover with independent suspension, recolor its headlights, and test wheel rotation, steering, and rough-terrain handling.
• Copper Metropolis: Build a quadcopter companion that follows the player through the air, tilting to accelerate, brake, and move sideways.

I also walk through the toolbar and the design behind the workflow: let an AI agent build the structure and controllers, then use hands-on tools to sculpt, paint, and refine the result. The goal is a world where your creations are editable, programmable, and ready to play with.

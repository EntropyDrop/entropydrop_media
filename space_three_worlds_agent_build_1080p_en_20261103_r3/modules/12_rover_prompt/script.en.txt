Our second request to the agent focuses on ground physics: build a sand-colored four-wheel, off-road vehicle with a sturdy chassis, high ground clearance, and prominent headlights.

The heart of the vehicle is its suspension: dynamic chassis, four independently animated wheels, and spring-and-damper forces calculated at every contact point. The wheels must articulate over bumps with responsive acceleration, steering, braking, and lateral grip.

This independent raycast suspension delivers authentic mechanical behavior you can feel in every turn, far beyond cosmetic wheels that only spin in place.

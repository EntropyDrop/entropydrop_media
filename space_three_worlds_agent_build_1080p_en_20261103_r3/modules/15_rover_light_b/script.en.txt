Applying the amber accent and stepping back, the rover's visual character transforms immediately. Even at long camera distances, the vehicle's front heading is instantly recognizable.

This highlights the core gameplay philosophy of Space: every generated machine remains completely editable. You never need to reroll a hundred-word prompt just to tweak a headlight color—the AI lays down the engineering, and you shape the personality with your own hands.

Brush alters appearances, Modeling adds cosmetic detail, and underlying physics controllers govern motion. Keeping these boundaries modular gives you absolute creative freedom.

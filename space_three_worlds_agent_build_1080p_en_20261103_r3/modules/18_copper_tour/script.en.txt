Our final stop brings us to Copper Metropolis. Packed with soaring skyscrapers, this world presents an entirely different navigation challenge from open wilderness.

Copper highlights how effortlessly Space renders dense, multi-tiered urban architecture. Sharp horizontal rooflines make every subtle tilt and attitude change easy to read.

In this vertical cityscape, we explore an even more advanced gameplay mechanic: instead of manually piloting a vehicle, we ask the agent to create an autonomous companion drone that follows our every move.

The player becomes the moving target. Starting from a spacious rooftop terrace, we take to the air, giving our drone companion plenty of clear airspace to climb, follow, and hover beside us.

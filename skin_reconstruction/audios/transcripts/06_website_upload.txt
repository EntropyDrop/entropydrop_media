To make your own, open entropydrop dot com slash skin slash generate, and sign in. Choose Image Mode, then upload a reference image. PNG and JPG are supported. Start with one character that is easy to see, with a clear face, hairstyle, and outfit. A full-body image gives the system more information about the clothes and shoes. A portrait can still provide a reference, but it leaves more of the outfit unspecified. Once the image appears in the upload preview, you're ready to choose a model.

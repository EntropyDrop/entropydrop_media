In the preview, use Plane mode to check the standard skin appearance. Drag to look around the character, and switch to Walk to see it moving. Check the face, the sides of the head, the sleeves, and the back before deciding you're finished. If you want to adjust a few pixels, the Edit button opens the skin editor. When you're happy with it, click Download to save the skin as a PNG. That's the skin texture you can import into Minecraft, using the matching character model.

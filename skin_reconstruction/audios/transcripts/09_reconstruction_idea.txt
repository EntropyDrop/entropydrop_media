So, how does reconstruction work? There are two main steps. First, an image model creates Minecraft front and back views of the character, following a fixed layout. That gives us the appearance we want to recover. Then the reconstruction system works out where those colors belong on the skin texture. You can think of the texture as a flat pattern that wraps around the head, body, arms, and legs. The result is a sixty-four by sixty-four skin file. Rendering it on the player model lets us check how that flat pattern looks in three dimensions.

The tricky part is deciding which surface a color belongs to. Minecraft skins have a base layer and an outer layer. Hair, a hat, or a pair of glasses can cover what's underneath, so simply copying colors from the picture can put them in the wrong place. The system combines the known shape of the Minecraft model with learned visual cues to estimate the right surface and layer. The reconstruction approach also pays attention to edges that meet around the head, and checks colors by rendering the skin again. That helps us evaluate the connected three-dimensional result.
